# Character design prompts for ChatGPT

Goal: concept art in the spirit of the SMT II cover (`reference/lighthouse_smt2_cover.jpg`), clean enough to turn into 3D models with Tripo or Meshy.

## How to use it

1. **Use one ChatGPT chat** for the whole cast, so the style stays consistent.
2. **First message:** paste the style block below plus ONE character. Ask for one character per image, never a lineup.
3. **For each character:** paste their description, then iterate in plain words ("longer cloak," "mask smoother").
4. **Once you love a design,** ask for the **3D-ready version** (bottom of this file) and use that image in Tripo or Meshy.

## Style block (paste first, in a fresh chat)

The first version of this block produced ornate modern fantasy art: jewelry, gears, castles and lineups. This version says what to avoid.

```
One single character, full body, centered, on a plain pale gray-white background
with only soft mist. No scenery, no buildings, no castles, no halos, no symbols.

Style: 1990s Japanese video game box art, airbrush and ink. Bold clean linework,
smooth airbrushed shading, flat and graphic, high contrast. Palette: pale bone and
cream skin and cloth, washed olive-yellow tint, with a few LARGE solid black shapes
(thick plain leather straps, a black blindfold or band). Black areas are big, flat
and simple, not detailed.

Avoid: ornate fantasy, filigree, jewelry, gears, compasses, gold trinkets, lace,
parchment texture, modern digital painting, gacha or fantasy-MMO style.
```

If it drifts ornate, reply: *"Simpler. Fewer details. Bigger, flatter black shapes. Like a 1994 game manual illustration."*

## Act I cast

**Takeya (protagonist)**
```
A Japanese man in his early 20s, a rank-and-file soldier who just survived a massacre
in the desert. Lean, exhausted, haunted eyes, dark hair caked with sand. Long slate-gray
military coat, a faded red patch on the shoulder, scuffed boots, webbing and pouches.
Strapped to his left forearm with a heavy ballistic-nylon brace: a bulky silver
early-2000s PDA, like an oversized BlackBerry, with a glowing cyan screen and thick
cables running into a wrist cuff. A plain standard-issue handgun at his hip.
Full body, standing, neutral pose.
```

**The angel (opening scene)**
```
A tall, gaunt angel in plain white robes, wrapped by three thick black leather straps
across chest, waist and thighs. Face completely hidden behind a smooth, featureless
gold mask. Two folded white wings. Holding a plain straight sword. Streaks of gold
blood on the robes. Cold, still, unsettling.
```

**The Imp**
```
A small, wiry red imp demon, about one meter tall. Oversized grinning mouth with needle
teeth, short curved black horns, pointed ears, long thin tail with a barbed tip, clawed
hands and feet, ribs showing. Vicious and mischievous. Full body, crouched to pounce.
```

**The scout (first boss's summoner)**
```
An elite soldier-summoner from the rival sect. Long dark olive coat, hood up, a worn red
scarf, amber-glowing goggles hiding the eyes. A silver PDA device strapped to the left
forearm, like the protagonist's but more battered. Cold and professional.
Full body, standing.
```

**Orthrus (first boss)**
```
Orthrus, a huge two-headed hound from Greek myth. Muscular, coal-black body, two snarling
heads with glowing orange eyes, a spiked iron collar on each neck, a live green serpent
for a tail. Black chains trailing from the collars. Menacing.
Full body, side three-quarter view.
```

## The 3D-ready version (send this after a design is final)

```
Now make a 3D-modeling reference of exactly this character: full body, front view,
standing in a relaxed A-pose with arms slightly away from the body, neutral expression,
plain light-gray background, even flat lighting, no shadows, no fog, no effects,
nothing overlapping the body, entire figure in frame head to toe.
```

For the best 3D result, also ask for **side and back views** in the same pose. Tripo and Meshy can use several views at once.
